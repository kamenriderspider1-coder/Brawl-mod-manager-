Brawl Cloud

Your Brawl mod library, stored in your Google Drive.

No extra cloud storage, no API connection, and no account access shared with anyone. Open Drive yourself and manage your files there.

☁️ Your Google Drive

Brawl Cloud uses the regular Google Drive website. Sign in with your own Google account, then create a folder named Brawl Cloud.

Open Google Drive ↗

This button opens Google's normal Drive website. Brawl Cloud does not sign in for you, read your Drive, or upload anything automatically.

Move mods from Drive to your Wii

  1. In Google Drive, create or open your Brawl Cloud folder.
  2. Upload mod files you created or are allowed to use.
  3. When you want a mod on the Wii, download it from Drive to your computer.
  4. Copy the downloaded file onto your Wii SD card inside sd:/brawl-mods/. Create that folder if it is not already there.
  5. Put the SD card back in your Wii and open Brawl Mod Manager from the Homebrew Channel.

Download the separate Wii Homebrew Channel app package directly from its GitHub release.

Download Wii Manager ZIP ↗ Release details ↗

The ZIP contains the Wii manager package. Extract it and copy the app folder into sd:/apps/ on your Wii SD card.

Prototype notice: this release currently provides an SD-card file browser. It does not yet install or activate mods in Brawl.

What the Wii manager does

The Wii app scans the brawl-mods folder on your SD card and shows the files it finds. Download files from Google Drive manually, then copy them to the SD card.

Brawl Cloud is a simple guide and shortcut to your own Google Drive. It does not request Google permissions, use OAuth, or connect to a storage API.
