Privacy and storage

  • Files are stored in your own Google Drive under a Brawl Cloud folder.
  • The app requests the limited drive.file permission, not access to your entire Drive.
  • Your Google access token is held in memory only and is not saved in GitHub or browser storage.
  • The 13 GB target is a Brawl Cloud usage limit. Your Google account's storage is shared with Gmail and Google Photos.
  • Only upload mods you created or have permission to use and share.
